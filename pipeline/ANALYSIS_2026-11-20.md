## What went wrong
1. **The first 3 seconds.** Half or more of viewers leave immediately on every Reel. Openers like "Day 3: you voted..." or "Game day." give no reason to stay.
2. **The stats panel kills the video.** In 9 of 11 timed episodes the average stayer leaves while the stats bars / scoreboard are on screen (around 9-17 s).
3. **Nobody sees the vote.** The vote starts at 29-39 s; the average stayer is gone by ~13 s. That explains near-zero votes better than anything else.
4. **Too long.** v2 made episodes longer (47-49 s) while watch time stayed ~5-10 s.
5. **Rosie's hooks are the weakest** (skip 57% -> 61% -> 72%) and she has never had a comment.
6. **Carousels: 0 reach** every time. Stories reach only followers (Theo 21, others 0).

## What worked
- The v2 retention features lowered 3 s skip on every account (Theo 56% -> 46%, Malik 66% -> 49%, Horde 58% -> 53%).
- Theo's single-moment morning bonus: best % watched and the first shares.
- Theo is the only account with a real community (repeat voters ivannn_720, noahissobased, 5sta.r.angel; new: ej123445).

## Changes (template v3, from Nov 21)
- 20-28 s. Order: hook (s1) -> story event (s3) -> vote (s5). No stats panel, no scoreboard scene; numbers live in the HUD + stickers ("AURA +3", "GLOW-UP +6%").
- Frame 1 = the most dramatic beat as a sentence ("He shaved a bald patch into his own head."), then a 1-line recap, then "stay to the end, you pick tomorrow".
- Vote screen by ~18 s, shorter vote lines.
- Trash Horde: verified-fact Reels (~27 s) in the new structure, 5/day test.
