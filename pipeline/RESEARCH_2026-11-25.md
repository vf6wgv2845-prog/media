# Growth research, Nov 25 2026 (Boris: "get to 10K, then 100K+ views per Reel, fast")

Method: web research by 3 parallel research passes (case studies, distribution levers, retention craft) plus our own Windsor data. Vendor and blog numbers are marked (vendor). No source tested animated TTS story Reels specifically, so treat the production items as hypotheses to A/B test.

## Our position
- Our gate (59 Reels, Nov 19-24): skip <= 40% AND avg watch >= 10 s -> 800-1,800 views; otherwise ~100-250.
- Jess (1,400-1,800) is at the median for small accounts. Metricool 2026: accounts under 2K followers average TikTok 2,429 / Instagram Reels 1,484 / YouTube Shorts ~1,290 views per video. https://www.merca20.com/tiktok-is-the-best-platform-for-starting-from-scratch-small-accounts-get-nearly-2500-views-per-video/
- Skip-rate averages (Socialinsider 2026, brand Reels): 65.5% at 1-5K followers, 60.5% at 100K-1M. Our 33-41% on Jess is far better than that. Watch time after 3 s, sends and distribution are the gaps. https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/
- Average Reel watch time is 8.5 s (Metricool 2026, 24.3M posts). Jess is at 10.6-13.4 s. https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/

## What Instagram ranks on
- Mosseri (Jan 2025): "The top three signals ... are watch time, likes and sends"; "sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/
- Mosseri (May 2024): sends per reach "correlate more ... with overall reach than anything else"; "the rate is more important than the count." https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-says-post-share-rates-are-now-a-key-driver-of-reach/716540/
- Recommendation eligibility: no watermarks, original content (aggregators that repost 10+ times in 30 days lose recommendations; since Apr 2026 this covers photos and carousels too). https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality , https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator
- Engagement bait: Instagram (Jun 2024) flagged "asking people to comment with a specific word, number, or emoji" as gaming the system, which may affect recommendation. Facebook demotes "vote baiting" and "comment baiting". https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-single-word-ctas-longer-reels/718151/ , https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/
- AI: profiles featuring an undisclosed AI-generated *person* lose reach (Aug 31 2026). Nothing covers cartoon story accounts. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/

## Breakout cases (what they share)
- Fruit Love Island (TikTok, Mar 2026): 3M followers in 9 days, 300M+ views, ~10M per episode. Parodies a familiar format, viewers' votes visibly change the plot, comments are read aloud, fan ideas get written in, daily cliffhangers, 2-4 min episodes. It was also hit with "AI slop" backlash and mass reporting. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fruit_Love_Island , https://news.designrush.com/tiktok-series-fruit-love-island-ai-format-risk
- Chinese AI pet dramas: 1M followers in under 2 months with 59 s underdog-revenge episodes; one creator runs several accounts. https://www.scmp.com/news/people-culture/article/3313925/chinas-ai-generated-pet-dramas-take-internet-storm-one-amassing-100-million-views
- Serialized creators ("Day 25 of starting over..."): a repeated labelled premise, and every episode understandable on its own. https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/07/the-web-series-is-back-only-this-time-theyre-tiktoks
- Nutshell Animations: growth came with one animation every day (2022). https://www.tubefilter.com/2022/08/11/youtube-millionaires-nutshell-animations/
- @30daystofix (Boris's recordings): see ANALYSIS_30daystofix_2026-11-21.md.

## Length, volume, series
- Median views by length (Socialinsider H1 2026, 9.77M Reels): 1-30 s 4,700; 30-45 s 8,564; 45-60 s 10,374; 60-90 s 9,790. This is a correlation. https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/
- Posting frequency (Buffer): Instagram reach per post +24% at 10+ posts/week vs 1-2. On TikTok the 90th-percentile post rises from 3,722 to 14,401 views with frequency while the median stays ~500. https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/ , https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/
- Linked Reels (next-part button, Aug 2025): in-app only, no API. https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/21/instagram-now-lets-creators-link-multiple-reels-in-a-series/
- YouTube Shorts seasons/episodes (Sep 2026). https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-creators-shorts-series-tv-features/
- Trial Reels: 1,000+ followers (Jul 2025). One blog claims 200 for professional accounts (unverified). The API supports `trial_params`. https://socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/

## Production (retention)
- Meta/Toluna (Aug 2026): core message within 5 s = 1.7x more likely to perform; lo-fi beat polished. https://tolunacorporate.com/what-makes-short-form-video-ads-work-meta-toluna-study/
- Safe zones on 1080x1920 (Meta ad guidance): keep text out of the top ~270 px, the bottom ~670 px (y > ~1,248) and the right ~230 px over the bottom 40%. https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/
- AI voices: 31% of people accept them in short-form; "unnatural voices" are the #2 tell (55%). https://stratcom.academy/2026/03/06/consumers-notice-ai-generated-voices-but-still-trust-humans-more/ , https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260121875037/en/83-of-Consumers-Can-Spot-AI-Videos-36-Say-It-Lowers-Brand-Trust-According-to-Animotos-New-Report
- Music under voice: no evidence that it lifts reach (one small test, p = 0.87). The Instagram Audio API can attach licensed or trending audio to Reels. https://www.thatrandomagency.com/blog/do-trending-sounds-impact-instagram-reel-performance , https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/audio-api/
- Motion: no rigorous study. Practitioners recommend a visual event every 1.5-3 s.

## Distribution and money
- Collabs (Emplifi H1 2026): XS accounts' collab posts got 5x the interactions of their normal posts (a correlation). https://emplifi.io/resources/instagram-collaborations-boost-organic-engagement/
- Boosting: needs a professional account at least 30 days old and no copyrighted audio. Meta suggests at least $5 a day for 6+ days. No official evidence that boosting changes later organic reach. Vendor CPMs: Reels $4-8 in consumer verticals; country projections US $23, Brazil $4.20, India $2.60. https://blog.hootsuite.com/cannot-boost-on-instagram , https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026 (vendor)
- TikTok: most reach for tiny accounts, but no API posting here; no networks of similar accounts allowed. https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity/
